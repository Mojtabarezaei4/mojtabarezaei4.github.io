Six players.

Every time your team wins the serve back, everybody moves one spot clockwise. The system decides who sets and who attacks as the team turns. Pick one and watch it play out.

Who's who

S
Setter
Takes the second touch and sets up the attack.

Outside Hitter
Attacks from the left. Main passer.
MB
Middle Blocker
Blocks at the net, hits fast middle sets.
OPP
Opposite
Attacks from the right, across from the setter.
